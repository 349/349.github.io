---
title: "Psalterium"
lede: "The Psalms in the Latin of the Clementine Vulgate."
summary: "The Gallican psalter — 150 psalms and the canticles — individually accessible and navigable by number and by use."
---

The psalter as prayed in the traditional Roman office: the **Gallican** text of the Clementine Vulgate, in the old (Vulgate) numbering — so the Twenty-third Psalm is here **Psalmus 22**, *Dominus regit me*. The full 150, together with the canticles, are going up in verified batches; pointing for the Gregorian tones follows. Below you can enter by liturgical grouping or by number.
