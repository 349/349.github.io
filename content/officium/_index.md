The Hours of the traditional Roman Breviary, computed for **today** in your browser — the liturgical season, the day, and each Hour's psalms drawn straight from the [Psalter]({{< relref "psalmi" >}}) according to the 1960 ferial distribution (taken from the Breviarium Romanum). **Compline** is fully assembled with its Ordinary — hymn, chapter, *Nunc dimittis*, collect, and the seasonal Marian antiphon; the other seven Hours show today's correct psalms, with their proper antiphons, chapters, hymns, and collects being wired in next. Append `?date=YYYY-MM-DD` to see any other day.
