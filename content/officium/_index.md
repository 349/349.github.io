The Hours of the traditional Roman Breviary (1960 rubrics), computed for **today** in your browser — the liturgical season, the day, and each Hour's psalms drawn straight from the [Psalter](/psalmi/) according to the Pius X ferial distribution. Append `?date=YYYY-MM-DD` to see any other day.

**Compline** is fully assembled with its Ordinary — the weekday-proper psalms and antiphon, hymn, chapter, short responsory, *Nunc dimittis*, collect, and the seasonal Marian antiphon — and its texts have been checked against *Divinum Officium* (Rubrics 1960). The other seven Hours currently show today's correct ferial psalms and the Ordinary; their **propers** (proper antiphons, chapters, collects, and the feast-of-the-day) are still being built.

> This is a **study and reference aid**, not an approved liturgical book. It does not yet compute feasts, ranks, or commemorations, so it should **not** be relied on to fulfil the obligation of the Office — for that, use approved books and the current Ordo. Compline is the most complete Hour; treat the rest as work in progress.
